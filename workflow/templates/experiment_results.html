Methodology, Queueing Flow & Statistical Rigor

Transparency & Explainability

Queueing & Arrival Model

  • Arrival Process: Poisson point process calibrated to peak clinic intake (λ = {{ sim_config.arrival_rate_per_hour }} cases/hour).
  • Cohort Uniformity: All 3 experimental modes process the exact same sequence of N = {{ sim_config.num_cases }} cases (seed = {{ sim_config.random_seed }}).
  • Specialist Routing: Fully simulated through the real routing engine code (subspecialty matching + capacity ceiling + priority-weighted load balancing).
  • Dynamic Starvation Term: Waiting time term (+0.05 pts/min capped at 20 pts) prevents routine study starvation.

Review Time Modeling & Overrides

  • Base Review Distribution: Lognormal distribution parameterized by anatomical modality and clinical priority (σ = 0.30).
  • Relevance Modulation: CBIR reduction factors (δcbir = {{ sim_config.cbir_time_reduction_factor * 100 }}%, δrerank = {{ sim_config.cbir_rerank_time_reduction_factor * 100 }}%) are scaled dynamically by retrieval precision (Precision@10).
  • Measured Data Override: If empirical timings are gathered from radiologist readers and placed at outputs/measured_review_times.csv, they automatically supersede the synthetic model.
  • Paired Hypothesis Test: Non-parametric paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test and 95% bootstrap confidence intervals confirm statistical significance without assuming normal variance.
